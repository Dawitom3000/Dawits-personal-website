About Dawit

Dawit A. Feleke is an Ethiopian project manager, entrepreneur, and creative leader who specializes in turning complex, undefined ideas into structured, high-impact results.

Dawit works at the intersection of strategy, execution, and creativity. With experience spanning international education, media production, youth sports, and entrepreneurship, he has consistently taken on environments where systems do not exist and built them from the ground up. From leading multicultural teams in Europe without a shared language to building a profitable travel consultancy from scratch and professionalizing a fast-moving music label, his strength lies in simplifying complexity and delivering results under pressure. He is comfortable owning the full chain from planning and budgeting to execution and final delivery while keeping teams aligned and focused.
