Education technology · Product delivery

Future Quest

Took an office-based education service digital, creating a platform used by students in more than 30 countries.

Role
Product Project Lead
Period
2022–2024
Organization
Future Quest · Headway Travel Firm
Areas
Product · Technology

01 · Challenge

Headway's scholarship, study, and visa services depended on students visiting an office. That limited access and made it harder for people outside Addis Ababa to discover suitable opportunities.

The opportunity was to translate a hands-on service into a clear digital experience without losing the guidance students relied on.

02 · My role

I led the product project from early development through launch, connecting real student workflows with the platform structure, opportunity discovery, consultations, and remote application journeys.

  • Product planning
  • Service-to-product translation
  • Team coordination
  • Launch delivery
  • Content and workflow definition

03 · Process

Make the work visible.

01

03

Led the team from development in 2022 through platform deployment in 2024.

04 · Execution

A searchable remote service platform for scholarships, education pathways, study materials, consultations, and application support. Students can explore opportunities and begin their journey without travelling to the Headway office.
