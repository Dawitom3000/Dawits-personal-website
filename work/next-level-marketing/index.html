01 · My contribution

I developed and launched the company website while serving as Head of Marketing and Communications, coordinating content, structure, and public delivery. My management responsibilities supported the website’s product and content decisions.

02 · Purpose & user journeys

Visitors need to understand the agency’s services, assess past campaign work, and reach the team. The application organizes the company archive into project stories, capability pages, experience, and direct contact options.

03 · Website implementation

  • Next.js/React pages written in TypeScript, with Vinext/Vite build tooling.
  • Responsive navigation, campaign photography, gallery interactions, and reduced-motion support.
  • Per-page metadata, sitemap, robots instructions, structured data, and branded error routes.
  • Email, phone, and WhatsApp contact paths; the current implementation uses direct contact rather than a message-delivery backend.

04 · Challenge: a large archive, a clear path

A substantial archive can make a company site difficult to scan. The implementation separates campaign stories, capabilities, and experience while keeping shared navigation and reusable content data.

Direct email links keep contact simple, but rely on the visitor’s email handler. This is the current source behavior; an earlier README line about form delivery does not describe the final contact implementation.

05 · Testing & delivery evidence

The repository includes rendered-HTML tests for public routes, metadata, local media, navigation destinations, health responses, and direct contact links. Build configurations target Cloudflare-compatible output and Vercel; the company’s linked live site was reachable during review.

The production host and individual infrastructure responsibilities are not inferred from build options. This is a responsive company website with no verified native mobile companion.
