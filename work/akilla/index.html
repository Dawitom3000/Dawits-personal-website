01 · My contribution

I co-developed Aquila, contributing code and product design before its internal deployment. I translated operational and client requirements into workflows and supported adoption across an operations team that grew to around 40 people.

02 · The problem & the people

Eagle Point AI’s training-data operations needed visibility into project progress and performance. Aquila was developed as an internal workflow monitoring tool for the operations team.

The useful engineering context is the connection between the workflow and its users: delivery requirements had to become something staff could follow and managers could monitor.

03 · My development contribution

  • Contributed to the product design and code as part of the team developing Aquila.
  • Translated operational and client requirements into clear workflows.
  • Supported internal deployment and adoption for project and performance visibility.

04 · AI operations & evaluation context

In the same role, I directed dataset collection operations and analyzed incoming client projects to define scope and delivery requirements. Eagle Point AI works in AI training data and evaluation; my documented contribution is dataset operations and project intake.
